# MarketRisk-Lab References

1. **Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS).** "Minimum capital requirements for market risk" (d457, January 2019). The primary text for FRTB standard concepts, Expected Shortfall (ES), Stressed ES, Liquidity Horizons, and the P&L Attribution Test.
2. **Kupiec, P. (1995).** "Techniques for Verifying the Accuracy of Risk Measurement Models". *The Journal of Derivatives*, 3(2), 73-84. (POF Test).
3. **Christoffersen, P. F. (1998).** "Evaluating Interval Forecasts". *International Economic Review*, 39(4), 841-862. (Independence and Conditional Coverage Tests).
4. **Acerbi, C., & Székely, B. (2014).** "Back-testing expected shortfall". *Risk*, 27(11), 76-81. (Z1 and Z2 tests for ES backtesting).
5. **Vasicek, O. A. (2002).** "The Distribution of Loan Portfolio Value". *Risk*, 15(12), 160-162. (Single-factor asymptotic credit risk model).
6. **ISDA.** "Standard Initial Margin Model (SIMM) for Non-Cleared Derivatives". Methodology and risk weight tables.
7. **Hull, J. C. (2017).** *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* (10th ed.). Pearson. (Black-76 options pricing, bond duration/convexity).
8. **Engle, R. F. (1982).** "Autoregressive Conditional Heteroscedasticity with Estimates of the Variance of United Kingdom Inflation". *Econometrica*, 50(4), 987-1007. (GARCH foundations).
9. **J.P. Morgan & Reuters (1996).** *RiskMetrics—Technical Document*. (EWMA volatility updating).
